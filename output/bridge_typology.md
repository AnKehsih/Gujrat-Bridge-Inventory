# Bridge typologies for OpenSees modelling

Generated by `scripts/classify_bridge_typology.py` from the GAD master data (09.07.2026).
Each typology is a group of bridges that can share one parametric OpenSees model:
**superstructure type | number of spans | governing (longest) span**.

- 10,455 bridges were classified into 142 typologies.
- 7,689 bridges (74%) fall in 110 fully defined typologies.

- The full list, and the typology of every bridge, is in `bridge_typology.xlsx`.

Columns: *assumed* = superstructure not written, taken as an RCC solid slab because the span is 12 m or less. *Masonry / RCC sub.* = substructure material where the text states it. The era columns count bridges by construction year: Pre-1980, 1980–2001, 2002–2014 (after the 2001 Bhuj earthquake) and 2015 onwards.
